How it works

How it works

  1. Pick a prompt for the job you need right now — a post, a hook, a poll, a growth idea.
  2. Replace every [bracketed item] with your own details. [topic] becomes “English vocabulary”, [audience] becomes “adult beginners”.
  3. Paste it into your AI chatbot and read the answer. If it is too long or too formal, reply with one short correction — “Shorter, under 100 words.”
  4. Edit before you post. Check every fact, delete anything that does not sound like you, add one detail only you could know.

One prompt, one job. That is the rule the whole pack is built on.

Prompt #1 — Pick a channel niche that can last a yearFree sample

Choose a niche narrow enough to stand out but wide enough to post about for a year.

Act as a Telegram channel strategist. I want to start a channel about [topic]. My audience is [audience, e.g. beginner English learners aged 18-30]. I can post [number] times a week and I know a lot about [your strengths]. Give me: (1) 3 narrower niche angles inside [topic], each with a one-line promise to subscribers; (2) for each angle, 5 example post topics I could write without research; (3) your pick for the angle most likely to still have fresh topics after 12 months, with one reason why. Format as a short table, then your pick.

Example: Topic: English vocabulary. Audience: adult learners. Pick: 'One word a day with a real example sentence and a 10-second quiz' — narrow, repeatable, never runs out of words.

Prompt #13 — Generate 30 specific post ideasFree sample

A month of specific post ideas, so you never stare at a blank screen.

Act as a content planner for a Telegram channel about [topic] for [audience]. Give me 30 post ideas, grouped into 6 themes with 5 ideas each. Every idea must be specific enough to write in under 30 minutes — a vague idea like 'talk about grammar' is not allowed; a good one is '5 words people mix up at work, with one example each'. At least 5 of the 30 ideas must be interactive (poll, quiz, question, challenge). Number the ideas 1-30.

Example: Ideas for a vocab channel: '10 words for job interviews', 'words native speakers actually shorten', 'English for the gym'.

Prompt #31 — Write a standard channel postFree sample

The core post format: one idea, one example, one question — the structure behind most good channel posts.

Act as a Telegram copywriter. Write a channel post about [topic] for [audience]. The post must: (1) open with one strong line, (2) teach exactly one thing, (3) include one concrete example, (4) end with one question for the readers. Keep it under 150 words. Use short paragraphs (max 2 lines each) and bold for the key phrase only. Add at most 2 emojis. Tone: [friendly / professional / funny]. Do not use hashtags.

Example: Post: 'The word "although" is easier than you think. Although it was raining, we went out. Try it: write one sentence with "although" below.'

Prompt #51 — Write 10 hooks for one postFree sample

The first line decides whether anyone reads the second line. Ten options beat one guess.

Act as a Telegram copywriter for a channel about [topic] for [audience]. Here is a post I want people to read: [paste your post or its topic]. Write 10 different first lines (hooks) for it, each under 12 words. Use a mix of these types: a question, a surprising fact, a bold statement, a relatable problem, and a promise of something useful. Number them 1-10. Do not use clickbait that the post cannot deliver — every hook must be honest about what the post contains.

Example: Hooks for a vocab post: 'Stop saying "very good".' / 'You have been using this word wrong.' / '3 words that make you sound fluent.'

Prompt #63 — Write a poll postFree sample

A poll is the lowest-effort engagement — one tap — so the question must be worth that tap.

Act as a Telegram content creator for a channel about [topic] for [audience]. Write a poll post about [subject]. Give me: (1) the poll question (under 20 words), (2) 4 answer options (each under 10 words, all realistic — no joke options unless the channel is funny), (3) the post text that goes above the poll (under 60 words, saying why the question matters), (4) one line telling me what I will learn from the results. Remember Telegram polls allow up to 10 options, but 4 is usually best.

Example: Poll: 'How do you learn new words?' — A) Flashcards B) Reading C) Watching videos D) Talking to people.
